Grocery Store, refreshed

Admin screens that are designed but not built yet. Screens already in the app have been removed from this page.

Zones & slots

The zone list is one compact panel: each zone on one line with its details underneath, and a green dot for live. The zone editor puts Save at the top next to the zone name, so it no longer floats on its own. Details, the Live switch and towers are separate sections with dividers, and the money fields have a ₹ sign and stay a sensible width. Slots show the days as small letters, and the tip becomes a quiet line instead of a big yellow box.

Customers

Scrollbars everywhere are now thin and soft green (lighter on the dark sidebar), as in the zone list and table below. The search box sits in a toolbar instead of stretching into a tall empty card. Each customer shows initials, name and email; the phone has a WhatsApp button next to it; the address shows tower and flat first with the society underneath. Status is one pill (Phone confirmed, Not confirmed or Blocked), and Block moves into a "more" menu so it is not one click away. Tap the order count to see their orders under the row. Filters and page numbers match Products and Stock, and the table scrolls inside the screen.
