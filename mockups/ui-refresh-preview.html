Grocery Store, refreshed

Admin screens that are designed but not built yet. Screens already in the app have been removed from this page.

Shop pages on every screen size

Every frame below is the same responsive page at a different width. Text stays the same size on every screen: page titles 16px on phones and 18px on a computer, product names 13.5px medium, prices 15px, product title 19px, nothing heavier than 600. Phones and tablets hide the page scrollbar (scrolling still works). Computers use a wider page (up to 1320px) with more items per row, so there is less empty space at the sides.

Home

Phones keep the green header and one offer card. Only on a computer the offer becomes a banner slider (three banners that slide by themselves, with arrows and dots), next to a card with the next three delivery slots; categories show 6 across and products 6 across, with a second Daily essentials row.

Category

Phones get filter chips under the page bar and two products per row with the cart bar floating above the tabs. On a computer every category is listed on the left, with five products per row.

Product

Phones keep the big picture, pack choices and an Add to cart bar at the bottom. On a computer the picture stays on the left while you scroll the details on the right, and a You may also like row fills the page below.

Cart

Address, items, delivery slot and bill in that order on a phone, with Place order at the bottom. On a computer the bill and Place order stay in a panel on the right; slots sit three across and a Forgot something? row fills the space below.

My orders

Filter chips, then one card per order with status, item pictures, date, total, View details and Order again. Three columns on a computer.

Order details

A progress line from Placed to Delivered and the items on the left; the bill, the address and a WhatsApp help button in a panel on the right on a computer.

Profile

Name and contact, order alerts and sign out on the left; the delivery address and recent orders on the right on a computer.

Address

Use my current location sits at the top, and boxes it fills turn light green with "Filled from your location" so customers check them. On a computer the form sits beside a taller map with the pin.

Add product

Cancel and Add product move to the top, next to the title, so they are always in reach. The form sits on the left in two cards; a narrow column on the right holds the photo (now possible while adding), the Show in the shop switch, and a live preview of the product card exactly as customers will see it. Each pack size shows its discount as soon as MRP and price are filled.

Edit product

The same layout. The title shows whether the product is in the shop. Stock is a read-only number with an Adjust link to the Stock page, since stock changes are logged there. Each pack size has its own show switch and a remove button; the preview shows the first pack with its discount badge.

Bulk upload

Three step cards across the page instead of one narrow box. Step 1 lists every column in the template (dashed ones are optional). Step 2 gives the rules that cause most mistakes. Step 3 is a drop area; once a file is chosen it shows the file name and row count with one Upload button. Results come back as a green Saved line (naming any new categories), or a red list of problem rows with the fix written out.
